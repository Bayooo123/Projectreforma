§ 1The Firm

Counsel for businesses, investors and the people behind them.

We advise businesses, investors, financial institutions, entrepreneurs, and private clients across a range of legal matters.

Our focus is commercial dispute resolution, litigation, arbitration, insolvency, and wills and estate planning. We are committed to protecting our clients’ interests, resolving complex disputes, and delivering solutions that create lasting value.

Our Mission
To deliver high-value, commercially grounded legal solutions that address our clients’ most complex challenges, protect their interests, and create sustainable value.

§ 2What We Do

Disputes resolved. Risk managed. Opportunities seized.

We solve complex legal and commercial challenges for businesses, entrepreneurs, investors, and individuals. Through strategic thinking and commercially grounded advice, we help clients navigate disputes, manage risk, protect their interests, and seize opportunities.

  • 2.1

    Commercial Dispute Resolution

    Resolving disagreements between businesses, shareholders and counterparties with an eye on the commercial outcome, not only the legal one.

  • 2.2

    Litigation

    Representation before Nigerian courts, from pre-action strategy and interim relief through trial and appeal.

  • 2.3

    Arbitration

    Advice and advocacy in domestic and international arbitration, including enforcement of awards.

  • 2.4

    Insolvency

    Guidance for creditors, directors and companies through restructuring, receivership, winding up and recovery.

  • 2.5

    Wills & Estate Planning

    Wills, trusts and succession arrangements that protect families and preserve what has been built.
