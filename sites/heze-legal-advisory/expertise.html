Practice area

Commercial Dispute Resolution

Disputes cost businesses time, money and relationships. We help clients assess their position early, choose the right forum, and pursue the outcome that serves their commercial interests, whether through negotiation, mediation, arbitration or the courts.

  • Contract and supply disputes
  • Shareholder and joint venture disputes
  • Debt recovery and enforcement of judgments
  • Negotiated settlements and mediation
  • Pre-dispute risk reviews
  • Contract and clause audits
Practice area

Litigation

When a matter must be decided in court, preparation decides the outcome. We build each case from the evidence up, secure interim protection where it is needed, and present our clients’ position with clarity at trial and on appeal.

  • Commercial and contractual claims
  • Interim and injunctive relief
  • Banking and finance litigation
  • Property and land disputes
  • Fundamental rights enforcement
  • Appeals
Practice area

Arbitration

Arbitration offers confidentiality, expertise and finality, but only when the process is managed well. We advise from the drafting of the arbitration clause through the hearing to the recognition and enforcement of the award.

  • Drafting arbitration and dispute resolution clauses
  • Domestic and international commercial arbitration
  • Emergency and interim relief
  • Challenges to arbitral awards
  • Recognition and enforcement of awards
  • Mediation and settlement agreements
Practice area

Insolvency

Financial distress calls for quick, clear decisions. We advise creditors on protecting their position and recovering what they are owed, and help directors and companies consider rescue options before value is lost.

  • Creditor strategy and debt recovery
  • Receivership and administration
  • Company voluntary arrangements
  • Restructuring and schemes
  • Winding up and liquidation
  • Directors’ duties in financial distress
Practice area

Wills & Estate Planning

Thoughtful planning protects families from uncertainty and conflict. We prepare wills and trusts that reflect our clients’ wishes, plan the succession of family businesses, and guide families through the administration of estates.

  • Wills and codicils
  • Trusts and family arrangements
  • Succession planning for business owners
  • Probate and letters of administration
  • Estate administration
  • Inheritance and estate disputes
